Circadian clock genes of Elysia timida

The 47 clock genes catalogued in E. crispata were searched for in ASM4364404v1, the chromosome-level assembly of Elysia timida (754 Mb). For each gene, this page gives its position in the assembly, whether it is complete, and the protein it encodes. The sequenced specimens came from Cadaqués, Spain.

Genes found

Of the 47 genes, 44 are intact, and one more is complete but contains a frameshift or stop codon in the assembly sequence (Timeout). One is partial or fragmented (r-opsin-like 1), and one was not found (Go-opsin 1).

Identification

No gene annotation is available in GenBank for this assembly, so all 46 gene models were obtained by aligning the E. crispata proteins to the genome. Each gene model was accepted only if it passed a reciprocal best-hit test against all E. crispata proteins, which distinguishes orthologues from paralogues. The proteins are 59 to 100% identical to their E. crispata orthologues (median 87%).

Limitations of the assembly

This is a chromosome-level assembly. Because all gene models were derived from the E. crispata proteins, regions that are poorly conserved or that are absent from the E. crispata model may be missing from the models shown here.

A gene model is classed as intact when it covers at least 90% of the alignment positions that are present in most species and its coding sequence in the assembly contains no frameshift or stop codon. Models obtained by aligning the E. crispata protein may lack poorly conserved regions at the ends of a protein.

Genes searched for but not found

The seven clock-associated genes that were not found in E. crispata were also searched for in this genome, with the same Drosophila and mammalian query proteins. None of them was found.
